## Elevator pitch (one paragraph)

**Drive a Car Simulator** is a Roblox highway simulator where you earn coins on every run, hatch pets for bonuses, rebirth for permanent power, and unlock four themed worlds from Grasslands to Neon City. Phase 4 doubles down on feel-good polish: guided first sessions, achievements with a trophy case, milestone celebrations, and a HUD tuned for phones and desktop—plus bonus coins when you roll with Roblox friends in the same server. It’s built for players who want a long-session grind with satisfying feedback at every mile.

## Five verified facts (no CCU or revenue claims)

Use these as-is for factual grounding. Do **not** add concurrent player counts, revenue, rankings, or “#1” claims unless Engineering publishes verified figures.

1. **Four worlds, one progression arc** — Players start in **Grasslands** and unlock later worlds in order using **coins, gems, or crystals** (exact costs and currencies match the in-game World panel and live `WorldConfig`; see [`PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md`](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §3 for the documented snapshot).
2. **Phase 4 is a polish pass** — Shipped emphasis includes **guided first runs**, **achievements + trophy case**, **milestone celebration feedback**, **notification/HUD layering**, and **calmer loading** messaging—aligned with the feature bullets in [`StorePageCopy_Phase4.md`](StorePageCopy_Phase4.md).
3. **Progress is account-based** — Player data is stored on **Roblox’s servers (DataStore)**; the game also **auto-saves about every 2 minutes** while you play ([`PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md`](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §1).
4. **Social bonuses are explicit in-game** — **Roblox friends in the same server** grant **tiered bonus coins** on runs; **community group** supporters can receive **bonus payouts** on runs when configured—rates are **defined in live game config** when published ([`StorePageCopy_Phase4.md`](StorePageCopy_Phase4.md), [`PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md`](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md)).
5. **Monetization is platform-standard** — **Game passes** and **developer products** are sold **by Roblox**; refunds and billing follow **Roblox’s policies**, not an in-game refund desk ([`PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md`](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §2).
