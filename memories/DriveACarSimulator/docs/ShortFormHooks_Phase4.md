# Short-form hooks — TikTok / Shorts (Phase 4)

**Purpose:** Ultra-short **opening hooks** for vertical video (not full scripts). Pair with b-roll from in-game capture.  
**Tone alignment:** `docs/StorePageCopy_Phase4.md` (POLA-370) — premium simulator, dopamine-forward, reward clarity; `docs/marketing/Phase4_WeeklyCreatorUpdateBundle_POLA400.md` (POLA-400) — friend/group bonuses, HUD readability, streak warmth, notification lane polish.  
**Constraints:** No unverified features; no “whale” jargon; claims limited to documented Phase 4 pillars (onboarding, achievements/trophy case, milestones, HUD/safe-area, friend & group bonuses, loading tips, pets, rebirth, worlds, leaderboard as *general* grind surface).

---

## On-screen text vs voiceover (safety)

| Approach | When to use |
|----------|-------------|
| **On-screen text (burned-in)** | Short punchy lines only; avoid tiny type; no promises of specific % or SKU pricing; no “live now” monetization claims (per POLA-95 guardrails in store copy). Prefer **feature-feel** words: clearer, smoother, juicier, readable. |
| **Voiceover** | Full hook + light CTA (“link in bio,” “search the game,” “bring a friend”); can repeat the emotional beat of the on-screen line without adding new mechanical claims. |

**Safe combo:** Big text = **one** verified hook idea; VO expands mood (crew, grind, flex) without inventing numbers or unreleased systems.

### Hook 1 — Friend bonus clarity

**Hook (118 chars):**  
`Invite your crew—Roblox friends in the same server bump your run coins, and the HUD finally shows the bonus clearly.`

- **Shot A:** Close-up of driving HUD with friend-bonus chip visible; thumb briefly taps safe area (no UI spam).
- **Shot B:** Cut to two player cars side-by-side on the road; quick punch-in on payout or bonus line (actual UI from your build).
- **Shot C:** End card: game title + “Bring friends” (no prices); optional pet silhouette from your capture.

**Text vs VO:** On-screen: first clause only (“Invite your crew—bonus coins when friends share your server”). VO: full line for clarity.
