# Community guidelines (brief)

**Drive a Car Simulator** is built for players of many ages. These rules keep the game and our spaces fun, fair, and safe. We’re glad you’re here—follow them so everyone can enjoy the road.

---

## Be respectful

Treat other players and staff with kindness. **No harassment, hate, threats, slurs, or targeted bullying** in chat, display names, or anywhere we host community discussion. Disagree without attacking; walk away from drama instead of feeding it.

## Play fair

**No cheating or exploits.** Don’t use automation, bugs, or third-party tools to gain an unfair advantage, grief others, or manipulate leaderboards and economy systems. Normal gameplay only—if something feels like an unintended shortcut, don’t spread it; report it (see below).

## Report problems the right way

If you see behavior that breaks these rules or [Roblox’s Community Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Community-Standards), **use Roblox’s report tools in-experience** for serious or safety-related issues. For feedback and bugs, use **official** channels (group, Discord, or paths we publish)—don’t share passwords, don’t move payments off-platform, and don’t use reports to settle personal arguments. We review reports in line with platform rules; repeated or severe violations may lead to moderation or escalation.

---

## Creators, clips, and streams

If you make **videos, shorts, or streams** about the game, label sponsored or edited content honestly, avoid clickbait that promises impossible rewards, and **protect players’ privacy**—blur or crop **codes, sensitive UI (prices, IDs), and piles of usernames** when clips could be reused out of context. Keep chat and titles family-friendly; don’t encourage harassment, scams, or exploit hunting. We love seeing your runs—show the game at its best.
