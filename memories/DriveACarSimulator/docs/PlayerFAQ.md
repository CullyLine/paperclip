## Leaderboards & fair play

**Q: How do leaderboards work?**  
Leaderboards rank progress using normal gameplay on Roblox servers. We want competition to feel exciting, not stressful—play your best, and remember everyone’s on the same rules.

**Q: What counts as cheating for leaderboard purposes?**  
Anything that gives an advantage **outside** intended gameplay—exploits, automation, stat manipulation, or other abuse of the experience—can skew results and breaks the fair-play line in [`docs/CommunityRules.md`](CommunityRules.md).

**Q: What do you do if something looks wrong?**  
We review reports and take action when we can, in line with Roblox’s policies. We **don’t** promise a specific response time for every report.

**Q: Where do I report bugs, bad behavior, or scams?**  
Use **Roblox’s report tools** for serious or safety issues. For feedback the team can see, use **official** group or Discord channels listed in our community spaces—don’t share passwords or move payments off-platform. Details: [`docs/CommunityRules.md`](CommunityRules.md) and [`docs/PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md`](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §7.
