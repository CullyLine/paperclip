Welcome to Drive a Car Simulator—we’re glad you’re here.

Be kind. Treat players, creators, and staff with respect. Share clips, ask questions, and disagree without attacking people.

No harassment. Hate, slurs, threats, bullying, scams, and spam aren’t okay. If you’re unsure what “okay” means here, read our community rules summary in CommunityRules_Phase4 (repo: docs/CommunityRules_Phase4.md) and the pinned rules in this server when they’re posted.

Report problems. On Discord: use Report on a message, or message a moderator with a short summary and screenshots or message links if you can. For serious issues (threats, grooming, severe harassment, exploits affecting others), also use Roblox’s in-experience reporting so there’s a platform record—see ModerationEscalation_Phase4 (docs/ModerationEscalation_Phase4.md) for the full flow.

Everyone follows Roblox’s rules: Roblox Community Standards — https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Community-Standards

Thanks for helping keep this a fun, safe place to hang out.
