## In-game footer (short paragraph)

Leaderboards celebrate how far you’ve driven with fair, normal gameplay. Play nice, skip exploits or automation, and if something looks off, use Roblox’s report tools—everyone’s on the same rules under Roblox’s Community Standards. More: Community Rules & FAQ in our docs hub.

---

## Discord pin (short paragraph)

Compete on skill and time in-game—no exploits, harassment, or account nonsense. We want the board to stay fun for everyone; serious issues go through **Roblox reporting** first. Full expectations: `./CommunityRules.md` and `./PlayerFAQ.md` in the repo (and linked longer packs under `docs/CommunityRules_Phase4.md`).
