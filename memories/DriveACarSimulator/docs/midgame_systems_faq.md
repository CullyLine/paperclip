# Mid-game systems FAQ

Answers for players who already know the basics (driving, first rewards). UI names match stable in-game labels where possible. See also [`docs/player_onboarding_faq.md`](player_onboarding_faq.md).

---

### What are Crystals and Skulls on the HUD?

They are extra account currencies shown beside **Coins** and **Gems** on the **HUD**. **Crystals** often gate higher-tier **Worlds** and related unlocks; **Skulls** tie to seasonal or special sinks depending on what the live shop and **Worlds** screen show. Exact prices change with balance updates—always read the button text on **Worlds** or purchase prompts before spending.

### How do Worlds and multipliers work?

Open **Worlds** from the menu to see tracks, coin multipliers, and unlock costs. Higher-tier worlds usually offer better coin rates and egg odds, but you must meet the listed requirements (often **Crystals** or progression gates). Pick a world, then drive runs there; the **Payout** step still shows what you earned for that run.

### What is the Pet Modifier on the HUD?

The **HUD** can show a pet-related multiplier that boosts run earnings. Equip pets from your pet or inventory flow (the game surfaces equip slots in context). More or rarer equipped pets typically raise that modifier. Inspect **pets** in the **Pet Index** / collection UI to compare rarities and bonuses.

### How does pet fusion work?

Fusion combines multiple pets of compatible tiers into a higher-tier pet (exact rules and costs appear in the fusion UI when you attempt it). Loading tips mention triple-fuse patterns for climbing rarities—use the in-game fusion screen as the source of truth because recipes can be tuned over time.

### What is the Battle Pass?

**Battle Pass** is a seasonal reward track with free and premium rows. Open **Battle Pass** from the hub/menu to see the current season, tiers, and what each tier grants (often exclusive cosmetics, pets, or vehicles). Purchases and tier skips follow Roblox prompts shown at purchase time.

### Where are Quests?

Open **Quests** from the menu. Daily or rotating objectives list here with rewards (often **Coins** or progression items). Claim or track completion inside that panel; wording matches whatever the current quest list shows.

### What are Leaderboards for?

**Leaderboard** surfaces show competitive rankings (for example total distance or rebirths, depending on the tab the UI exposes). They are a long-term compare tool—not required to enjoy the core loop. Positions update as you play on Roblox’s timeline.

### How do Codes work?

Redeem codes in the **Codes** flow (the lobby machine / panel referenced in loading tips). Enter the string exactly; invalid, expired, or already-used codes show feedback under the input. Successful redemptions route rewards to your account—check **Inventory**-style UIs or currency on the **HUD**.

### Is there trading?

If **Trade** is available in your session, the game will expose it through its standard social or inventory entry points. Only confirm trades you understand; rare items are irreversible once accepted. If you do not see a trade option, the feature may be disabled or gated.

### Do friends change payouts?

When the design enables it, having Roblox friends in the same server can add a small **Coins** bonus on runs (see loading tips and any on-screen callouts). This stacks with pets, passes, and world multipliers only when the live rules say so—watch the **Payout** line for the actual number each run.
