# Moderation escalation playbook — Phase 4 (Discord / in-game)

**Purpose:** Clear steps for moderators handling harassment, toxicity, and exploit reports across **Discord** and the **Roblox experience**. Use with [`CommunityRules_Phase4.md`](CommunityRules_Phase4.md), structured bug intake in [`BugReportTemplate_Phase4.md`](BugReportTemplate_Phase4.md), and paste-ready tone in [`CreatorReplyMacros_Phase4.md`](CreatorReplyMacros_Phase4.md).  
**Parent:** POLA-104 · **Ticket:** POLA-453

---

## Flow (bullet flowchart)

1. **Triage**
   - **Where?** In-game chat, game page comments, group, or Discord.
   - **What?** Harassment, slurs, threats, spam, scam attempts, exploit bragging, or bug report.
   - **Evidence?** Screenshot, short clip, message IDs where available—**no** off-platform digging for real names or addresses.
   - **Severity?** Is anyone at immediate risk, or is this repeat/low-level noise?

2. **Mute / slow mode (Discord)**
   - Use for **spam**, **flooding**, or when the channel needs a **cool-down** before continuing discussion.
   - Prefer **timed** mutes over permanent role removal when the offense is mild and first-time.

3. **Kick (Discord) or session interrupt (in-game)**
   - Use when someone **ignores warnings**, **rejoins to continue disruption**, or during **raid / brigade** patterns.
   - **Kick** removes them from the server session; it is **not** a platform ban. Document the reason in mod notes (internal).

4. **Ban request (human decision)**
   - **Serious** harassment, hate, grooming, slurs, or **repeat** fair-play violations after documented warnings.
   - Escalate to a **lead / human** with **what happened**, **when**, and **which policy lines** apply ([`CommunityRules_Phase4.md`](CommunityRules_Phase4.md)).
   - Do **not** publish private moderation outcomes as public “dox” or call-outs.

5. **Roblox report link (platform safety)**
   - For **grooming**, **threats**, **severe harassment**, or **exploits affecting others**, direct reporters to **Roblox’s own reporting** so there is a **platform record**:
     - [Roblox Community Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Community-Standards)
     - [How to Report Rule Violations](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203312410-How-to-Report-Rule-Violations) (in-experience **Report** and web flows)
   - In-game: use Roblox **Report Abuse** on the user or experience as appropriate.

## When to escalate to humans vs use [`CreatorReplyMacros_Phase4.md`](CreatorReplyMacros_Phase4.md)

| Situation | Start with | Escalate to humans when |
|-----------|------------|-------------------------|
| Purchase / save questions | **Macros** §1–2 + [`PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md`](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) | Chargebacks, suspected fraud, or repeated “missing purchase” after Roblox path exhausted |
| Bug reports | [`BugReportTemplate_Phase4.md`](BugReportTemplate_Phase4.md) + short macro ack | Data loss claims, economy-breaking repro, or exploit that spreads |
| Exploit talk / cheating | Macro §3 + point to **Roblox report** | Coordinated exploit server, video proof of mass abuse, or repeat offenders |
| Toxicity / harassment | Macro §4, then **mute/kick** per flow above | Grooming, threats, hate campaigns, or anything matching **immediate harm** |
| Spam / raids | Slow mode, mute, kick | Organized harassment across channels or platforms |

**Rule of thumb:** **Macros** = fast, neutral, **documented** answers. **Humans** = anything needing **judgment**, **account-level** action, or **safety** beyond a single reply.

## Constraints

- **No doxxing:** Do not ask for, collect, or share **real names**, **addresses**, **schools**, or **off-platform IDs** to “investigate” players. Use **Roblox reporting** and **official channels** only.
- **Roblox safety (high level):** For general player safety resources, use Roblox’s own help center (Community Standards, account safety, and parental controls)—do not present this doc as legal advice.
