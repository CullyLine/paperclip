## What we expect

We want **Drivers** to have fun in a fair, friendly space. Treat others with respect, keep chat and names appropriate for all ages, and stick to normal gameplay—no cheating, exploiting, or ruining someone else’s run. When something goes wrong, we’d rather you **try again** or use official report paths than accuse other players in chat.

Serious or repeated problems may lead to in-game moderation or **platform reports**, consistent with Roblox’s rules—not a separate promise about how fast any single report is handled.

---

## Fair play

- **No unfair edges** — automation, exploits, or anything that skips intended gameplay undermines leaderboards and everyone else’s progress.
- **No harassment** — no hate, threats, targeted bullying, or pressure in chat, names, or community spaces we run.
- **No scams or phishing** — don’t ask for passwords, off-platform payment, or personal info.

---

## Where the rules come from

This experience lives on Roblox. The rules that apply to **every** game and account are in Roblox’s own documents—read them for the full picture:

- [Roblox Community Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Community-Standards)
