# Creator reply macros — Phase 4 (moderation + social)

**Purpose:** Fast, paste-ready replies for **creators and moderators** on game page comments, group posts, Discord tickets, and social DMs—**neutral, helpful, not defensive**. Each line is **≤280 characters** (Roblox/social-safe length).  
**Cross-links:** Facts align with [`PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md`](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) and expectations with [`CommunityRules_Phase4.md`](CommunityRules_Phase4.md).  
**Constraints:** No promises about **refunds**, **manual restores**, or **timelines** the team cannot guarantee.  
**Parent:** POLA-104 · **Ticket:** POLA-421

---

## How to use

- Pick **Variant A** or **Variant B** per situation; tweak names only if needed.
- Prefer **public** replies on the same channel when safety allows—reduces duplicate DMs.
- For **safety or grooming concerns**, do not debate—point to **Roblox reporting** and stop engaging.

---

## 1) Purchase issue (missing pass, charged but no item)

**Variant A**  
Thanks for letting us know. Purchases and billing go through **Roblox**—we can’t issue refunds or push charges here. Check Roblox purchase history; if a pass still didn’t show after **rejoin**, use Roblox purchase help. [Player FAQ](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §2.

**Variant B**  
Sorry this is frustrating. We **can’t** refund or force-deliver items from here—**Roblox** handles payments. Try **rejoining once**, then Roblox support if it’s still missing. Overview: [Player FAQ](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §2.

---

## 2) Lost progress (save gone, rollback, “I lost everything”)

**Variant A**  
Progress is saved on **Roblox servers** with periodic autosaves—see [Player FAQ](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §1. We **can’t** manually restore accounts from DMs. If something looks wrong, **rejoin** on the **same account** and give it a minute to sync.

**Variant B**  
We’re sorry this feels bad. Saves are handled by **Roblox DataStore** + in-game autosave—not something we can roll back by hand in chat. Try a **fresh rejoin**; avoid force-closing mid-purchase when possible. Details: [Player FAQ](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §1.

---

## 3) Exploit report (cheating, scripts, duping)

**Variant A**  
Thank you—exploits ruin the experience for everyone. Please **report through Roblox’s in-experience tools** with what you saw; don’t send downloads or links to hacks. We review alongside **fair-play** expectations: [Community Rules](CommunityRules_Phase4.md).

**Variant B**  
We take this seriously. Use **Roblox reporting** so there’s a record; we **can’t** verify or fix exploits from DMs alone. Exploits break our fair-play line—see [Community Rules](CommunityRules_Phase4.md) and [Player FAQ](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §7.

---

## 4) Toxicity (harassment, slurs, targeted trash talk)

**Variant A**  
Sorry you dealt with that. We expect **respect** and compliance with **Roblox Community Standards**. Please **mute/block** and **report** in-experience; we can’t moderate private DMs. Our baseline: [Community Rules](CommunityRules_Phase4.md).

**Variant B**  
Harassment isn’t okay here. **Report** the chat/username through Roblox; we **don’t** promise instant outcomes in replies, but we align actions with platform rules. Expectations: [Community Rules](CommunityRules_Phase4.md).

---

## 5) Request for codes (“code pls,” “leak codes”)

**Variant A**  
Codes are **redeemed in-game** (Codes panel), **once per account**, and expire—see [Player FAQ](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §6. We don’t DM unique codes; watch **official** posts for drops. Invalid codes won’t work.

**Variant B**  
We can’t generate or promise codes in thread—**server-checked**, **one-time** redemption. Use the **Codes** panel and follow **official** announcements. FAQ: [Player FAQ](PlayerFAQ_Phase4.md) §6.
