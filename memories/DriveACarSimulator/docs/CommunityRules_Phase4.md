# Community rules — Phase 4 (store, group, Discord)

**Purpose:** Trust-and-safety copy you can paste into the **Roblox game/group store** blurbs, **group** description or About, and **Discord** pinned rules. Tone is neutral and professional; it does not create legal duties beyond pointing players to Roblox’s own terms.  
**Parent:** POLA-104 · **Ticket:** POLA-412

---

## Community standard (paragraph)

We want everyone to enjoy the game in a positive, fair environment. Treat other players and staff with respect, keep chat and usernames appropriate for all ages, and use only normal gameplay—no cheating, exploiting, or attempts to harm others’ experience. Serious or repeated violations may be handled with in-game moderation or platform reports, consistent with Roblox’s rules. For the full rules that apply to every Roblox experience, see the [Roblox Terms of Use](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004647946) and [Roblox Community Standards](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313410-Roblox-Community-Standards).

---

## Short rules (bullets — paste under the paragraph or as a pinned list)

- **Respect others** — No harassment, hate, threats, or targeted bullying in chat, names, or group spaces.
- **Play fair** — No exploits, automation, or anything that gives an unfair advantage outside intended gameplay.
- **Keep it clean** — No scams, phishing, or trying to steal accounts, items, or personal information.
- **Stay on-topic** — Use official channels for feedback and reports; don’t spam or derail community threads.
- **Follow Roblox** — This experience follows Roblox’s Terms of Use and Community Standards; when in doubt, follow those.
