# Player onboarding FAQ

Plain-language answers for new players. UI names match in-game surfaces where they are stable. For progression past the basics—worlds, extra currencies, pets, Battle Pass, quests, and leaderboards—see [`docs/midgame_systems_faq.md`](midgame_systems_faq.md).

---

### How do I drive?

Use your platform’s normal Roblox movement controls (keyboard **W A S D** or **arrow keys** on PC; thumbstick on mobile/console). The **HUD** shows speed and run-related info while you are driving. Finish a run to bank rewards shown on the **Payout** flow.

### Does my progress save?

Yes. Progress is tied to your Roblox account and is saved on Roblox’s servers when you are online. If you play on another device with the same account, your progress should follow you. If something looks out of date, rejoin the experience after a stable connection returns.

### What are coins and how do I get them?

Coins are the main soft currency. You earn them by completing driving runs and through other activities the game surfaces (for example **Daily Reward** streaks or milestones). The **Payout** step after a run shows how many coins you earned that run.

### What can I spend coins on?

Typical sinks include **Egg Shop** eggs, **Garage** upgrades and vehicle-related spends, and other coin gates the UI labels in context. Open **Garage** or **Egg Shop** from the hub or menu to see current prices and options.

### How do vehicles and the Garage work?

You unlock and improve vehicles over time through the **Garage**. Exact stats and upgrade paths are shown on the vehicle cards and upgrade buttons there. If a button is locked, the UI usually states what milestone or level you still need.

### What are eggs and pets?

Eggs are opened from the **Egg Shop** (and related flows). Hatching gives **pets** that generally boost earnings or other run rewards depending on the pet. Pet details appear in collection or inventory style UIs when you inspect them.

### What is Rebirth?

**Rebirth** is a progression reset for long-term bonuses: you trade early progression for permanent multipliers or unlocks, depending on the current design. The **Rebirth** screen explains costs and what you keep versus what resets. Only rebirth when you understand the tradeoff shown on that screen.

### Where is the Daily Reward?

Open **Daily Reward** from the main hub/menu (wording on the button may vary slightly by layout). It tracks login streaks and grants rewards on eligible days. If you miss a day, streak rules shown in that panel apply.

### Where are Settings?

Open **Settings** from the main menu or pause-style menu. You can adjust audio and other options exposed there. Accessibility-related toggles appear in **Settings** when the game provides them.

### Where do I see achievements and trophies?

Use the **Trophy Case** entry (often from the **HUD** or hub). It lists achievements, progress, and filters so you can see what is locked versus completed.
